VICE SHORES

An open-world crime game in the sun-soaked city of Vice Shores. You are Dante “Dagger” Cole, a getaway driver fresh out of prison, working for the fixer Mara Quinn. Steal cars, outrun the Meridian PD, and pull off the Meridian Job.

8 story missions · wanted level 0–5 · police helicopter at 3 stars · 3 radio stations · saves locally

How to play

W A S D walk / drive   Shift run
E steal a car, get out, open the shop
Mouse aim   Click shoot (once you own a pistol)
F punch   Space handbrake
R change radio station   M mute
P / Esc pause   1–4 buy in the shop

Crimes raise your wanted level: the PD sends patrol cars, then officers, then a helicopter at 3 stars. Lay low and the heat cools off. Finish missions to earn cash, spend it at Vice Supplies, and mind your health — getting wasted drops you at the hospital minus 15% of your cash.

Paused

Vice Shores waits for no one — except right now.

Vice Supplies

Health, ammo and hardware. Cash only, no refunds.

Wasted

The Meridian PD got the better of you.

The Meridian Job — done

The vault is empty, the helicopter is gone, and Vice Shores is yours. The story is complete — free roam is open, missions can be replayed from the title screen.
