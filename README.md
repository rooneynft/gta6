**Meridian Heat** is a top-down open-world crime game set in the sun-soaked
coastal city of Port Meridian. You are Dante "Dagger" Cole, a getaway driver
fresh out of prison, working for the fixer Mara Quinn. Steal cars, outrun the
Meridian PD, run courier jobs, settle scores with the Harbor Kings, and pull
off the Meridian Job — all in one living city.

## How to play

Serve `game/` with any static server (`node serve.ts`, then open
http://localhost:8080) and play with keyboard and mouse. No network needed.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D / arrows | Walk / drive (steer while driving) |
| E | Steal / enter a car, get out |
| Mouse + click | Aim and shoot (once you have a pistol) |
| F | Punch |
| Space | Handbrake |
| P / Esc | Pause |
| M | Mute sound |

## What's in the city

- A drivable city: roads, traffic, pedestrians, a beach, docks and five districts
- Steal any car on the street; faster cars handle better and outrun the law
- A wanted system (0–5 stars): the Meridian PD sends patrol cars and officers
  who chase, ram and arrest you; lay low to cool off
- Five story missions with a fixer, a courier run, a dockside payback, a hot
  sports car and a final bank job — plus free roam after the story
- Combat on foot and from a car, cash pickups, health packs, car damage and
  explosions, a HUD with a live minimap, and a save that keeps your progress

Everything — the city, the characters, the story, the art and the sounds — is
original, drawn and synthesized for this game. No assets from any commercial
game are used.
