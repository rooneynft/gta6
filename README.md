**Vice Shores** is a 3D open-world crime game set in the sun-soaked coastal
city of Vice Shores. You are Dante "Dagger" Cole, a getaway driver fresh out
of prison, working for the fixer Mara Quinn. Steal cars, outrun the Meridian
PD, run jobs across the city, settle scores with the Harbor Kings, and pull
off the Meridian Job — all in one living 3D city.

## How to play

Serve `game/` with any static server (`node serve.ts`, then open
http://localhost:8080) and play with keyboard and mouse. No network needed.
Three.js is vendored under `game/vendor/`.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D / arrows | Walk / drive (steer while driving) |
| Shift | Run |
| E | Steal / enter a car, get out, open the shop |
| Mouse + click | Aim and shoot (once you have a pistol) |
| F | Punch |
| Space | Handbrake |
| R | Change radio station |
| M | Mute sound |
| P / Esc | Pause |
| 1–4 | Buy in the shop |

## What's in the city

- A 3D procedural city: roads, buildings, palms, a beach and the sea, a park, docks and eight districts
- Third-person on foot and driving, with a camera that follows behind you
- Steal any car on the street; the gold Comet GT is the fastest ride in town
- A wanted system (0–5 stars): patrol cars chase and ram you, officers chase you on foot, and a police helicopter shadows you from 3 stars; lay low to cool off
- Eight story missions with a fixer, courier runs, a dockside payback, a hot car, a shop errand, three stashes, a getaway and a final bank job — plus free roam after the story
- Combat on foot and from a car, cash and health pickups, car damage and wrecks, a HUD with a live minimap, speedometer, radio readout and mission tracker
- Three procedural radio stations (synthwave, guitar rock, chill), synthesized live with WebAudio — no audio files
- Vice Supplies: buy medkits, ammo, a pistol and body armor with mission money
- A save that keeps your cash, health, mission and position in the browser

Everything — the city, the characters, the story, the art and the sounds — is
original, built and synthesized for this game. No assets from any commercial
game are used.
